Dr. Krishan Singh is one of India's most seasoned Industrial Relations practitioners — a professional who has spent over three and a half decades at the intersection of labour law, union dynamics, and organisational leadership.

Across tenures with leading Indian conglomerates including SRF Limited, he has built a reputation for de-escalating complex labour disputes, crafting durable long-term wage settlements, and institutionalising IR frameworks that outlast individual tenures.
